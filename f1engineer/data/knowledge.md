## Braking points and trail braking
Brake in a straight line at maximum pressure, then release progressively as you turn in (trail braking) so the front tyres keep load and the car rotates. Braking too early costs more time than braking slightly late: lose 10 m of braking and you lose roughly 0.05-0.1 s per corner. Use fixed markers (boards, kerb starts, shadows) and move the point 5 m at a time.

## Lock-ups
A lock-up happens when brake force exceeds front tyre grip. Fixes: less peak pressure, bias rearwards (lower front %), release brake earlier as you add steering, avoid braking on kerbs. Locking flat-spots tyres and makes the car run wide.

## Keyboard driving technique
Keyboard throttle, brake and steering are digital (0 or 100%). Tap throttle in short pulses to hold mid-corner speed instead of coasting, tap the steering instead of holding it, and use traction control medium and ABS while learning. Lower brake pressure (85-90%) makes full-brake taps less likely to lock. In Controls set steering saturation lower and linearity higher for smoother digital steering.

## Controller technique
Squeeze triggers instead of snapping them. Unwind the steering before applying full throttle. Small steering inputs at high speed; the car pivots on the brakes.

## Corner phases
Entry: braking and turn-in. Mid: minimum speed at the apex. Exit: throttle application and track-out. The exit onto a long straight matters most because the speed is carried down the whole straight. Sacrifice entry speed for a better exit before long straights (e.g. Silverstone Vale/Club, the Loop onto Wellington Straight, Stowe).

## Racing line
Outside-apex-outside maximises radius. Late apex for corners before straights. Missing the apex by 1 m forces a wide exit and later throttle.

## Understeer
Front tyres lose grip first; the car runs wide. Low speed fixes: softer front anti-roll bar, softer front suspension, more on-throttle diff opening. High speed: more front wing. Driving: brake earlier and in a straighter line, slower entry, rotate on trail brake.

## Oversteer
Rear loses grip; car spins. Exit oversteer: lower on-throttle differential, softer rear anti-roll bar, gentler throttle, higher gear. High-speed: more rear wing. Entry oversteer: more front brake bias, higher off-throttle diff.

## F1 2020 setup parameters
Front/rear wing 1-11 (downforce vs top speed). Differential on-throttle 50-100% (lower = more open = less wheelspin, better rotation on exit), off-throttle 50-100% (higher = more stable on entry). Camber: more negative = more cornering grip, more tyre heat. Toe: front toe-out = sharper turn-in; rear toe-in = stability. Suspension and anti-roll bars 1-11 (softer = more mechanical grip, slower response). Ride height lower = more downforce, risk bottoming. Brake pressure 50-100%; brake bias 50-70% front. Tyre pressure higher = less rolling resistance and more heat but less grip.

## Tyres
F1 2020 tyres work best with surface temps roughly 90-105 C (soft lower end). Wear above about 70% average falls off a cliff. Softs are fastest, hards last longest. Locking and wheelspin overheat tyres.

## ERS and fuel
ERS modes: none, medium, overtake (spend), hotlap. Use overtake on straights to attack or defend, harvest in the slow sections. Fuel mix lean saves fuel; lift-and-coast before braking zones saves fuel with little lap time cost.

## Race strategy
Pit when tyres approach the cliff or when the pit loss is reduced (safety car roughly halves it, VSC reduces it by around 40%). Undercut: pit first and use fresh-tyre pace when the gap ahead is smaller than the fresh-tyre advantage over one or two laps. Overcut works when tyres are not degrading and track is clear.

## Silverstone
High-speed track. Key corners: Maggotts-Becketts-Chapel (rhythm, minimal braking, commit), Copse (fast right, flat or a lift), the Loop (slowest corner, get the exit onto Wellington Straight), Village, Brooklands-Luffield (long double apex, patience on throttle), Stowe (heavy braking after Hangar Straight), Vale-Club (heavy braking, exit onto the pit straight is critical). Medium-high downforce setup.
